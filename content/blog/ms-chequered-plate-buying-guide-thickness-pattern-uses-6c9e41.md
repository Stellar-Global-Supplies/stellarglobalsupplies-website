# MS Chequered Plate Buying Guide: Thickness, Pattern & Uses

Mild steel (MS) chequered plate—also called tread plate or floor plate—has a raised surface pattern. It is commonly specified for flooring, ramps, stair treads and platforms where the surface design is part of the requirement.

Choosing a plate is not only about thickness. Confirm the intended use, support spacing, load, sheet dimensions, surface pattern, material specification and fabrication needs before ordering.

## What is MS chequered plate?

MS chequered plate is a mild steel plate with a raised pattern on one surface. The pattern may be diamond or lentil (teardrop), depending on the product.

The raised pattern can help provide traction, but it does not guarantee slip safety. Conditions such as oil, water, dust, footwear, cleaning and installation affect grip. Confirm the surface is suitable for the actual working environment.

## Listed specifications to check

The MS Chequered Plate product page currently lists these sourcing details. Confirm the required specification and availability for your order:

- **Material specification:** IS 3502 and IS 2062 are listed.
- **Base thickness:** 2.5 mm to 10 mm.
- **Pattern:** Diamond or lentil (teardrop).
- **Sheet size:** 1250 × 2500 mm or custom dimensions.
- **Surface:** Hot-rolled, mill finish.

The listed range is a starting point. Your drawing, project specification and load requirements should determine the plate thickness and dimensions.

## Where is tread plate commonly used?

Typical applications listed for MS chequered plate include:

- Industrial flooring and walkways.
- Loading ramps.
- Stair treads and access platforms.
- Vehicle floor plates.
- Marine decking, where the specified material and environment are suitable.

For any application involving a defined load, support spacing, exposure or safety requirement, have the plate specification checked by the responsible engineer or project team.

## How to shortlist the right plate

- **Pattern:** Specify diamond, lentil or the pattern shown on the drawing.
- **Thickness:** Confirm the base thickness—not only the raised pattern height—and match it to the design.
- **Sheet dimensions:** Specify standard sheet size or the exact cut dimensions required.
- **Material standard and grade:** Use the drawing or purchase specification to identify the required standard and grade.
- **Support and loading:** Share the load, span, support arrangement and use case when thickness selection is uncertain.
- **Surface and environment:** Consider moisture, chemicals, oil, outdoor exposure, coating and cleaning.
- **Fabrication:** Confirm cutting, welding, bending, edge finishing and installation requirements before procurement.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between chequered plate and plain MS sheet?

Chequered plate has a raised pattern on one surface. Plain MS sheet has a flat surface. Choose based on the application, surface requirement, design and fabrication specification.

### Which thicknesses are listed for MS chequered plate?

The product page lists base thicknesses from 2.5 mm to 10 mm. Confirm the exact thickness and availability when requesting a quote.

### Which patterns are available?

The listed patterns are diamond and lentil (teardrop). Confirm the required pattern before ordering.

### Can custom sheet dimensions be requested?

The product page lists 1250 × 2500 mm and custom sizes. Share your dimensions and quantity so the supplier can confirm.

### Is chequered plate slip-proof?

The raised pattern may help traction, but the plate is not guaranteed slip-proof. Surface contamination, moisture, wear and installation conditions can affect grip. Confirm suitability for your safety requirements.
