# MS Flats Buying Guide: Sizes, Grade & Uses

Mild steel (MS) flat bars are rectangular steel sections used in fabrication, brackets, machine bases, frameworks and other industrial components. When sourcing MS flats, start with the drawing or purchase specification and confirm the dimensions, grade, length, quantity and application.

## What are MS flats?

MS flats are solid rectangular mild steel sections. They are commonly selected for fabrication work where a flat section is required. The width and thickness determine the section dimensions; the material grade and application requirements should be confirmed before purchase.

## Listed sizes and specifications

The MS Flats product page currently lists the following range. Confirm the exact size, grade and availability for your order:

- **Grade:** IS 2062 E250.
- **Width:** 12 mm to 300 mm.
- **Thickness:** 3 mm to 50 mm.
- **Length:** Standard 6 m or cut-to-size requirement (confirm service and availability).
- **Surface:** Mill finish.

Use the listed range as a starting point, not as a substitute for your project specification.

## Common applications

MS flats may be specified for:

- Fabrication brackets and supports.
- Machine bases and frames.
- Grating bars.
- Architectural frameworks.
- Agricultural equipment.

The correct section depends on the drawing, load, environment, joining method and design requirements.

## How to select the correct MS flat

- **Width and thickness:** Match the dimensions on the drawing or purchase specification.
- **Grade:** Confirm IS 2062 E250 or the grade specifically required by the project.
- **Length:** Specify standard length or requested cut length; confirm the service before ordering.
- **Surface:** Confirm mill finish or any additional surface treatment required.
- **Application:** Share how the section will be used so sourcing can be aligned with the requirement.
- **Quantity:** Provide pieces or total length and any packaging/handling details.
- **Documents:** Specify inspection or material documents required with the order.

## RFQ checklist

To request a quotation for MS flats, share:

- Grade and specification.
- Width and thickness.
- Length and quantity.
- Surface finish.
- Application or drawing.
- Delivery location and required date.
- Inspection or document requirements.

Clear dimensions and quantity help confirm the quotation details.
